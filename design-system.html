The rules for every title card, number callout and list on screen. Dark base, one green, big type, small system labels. If a frame has two accents, delete one.

Motion

Slow and soft // one ease
Ease
cubic-bezier(0.22, 1, 0.36, 1). Starts quick, then glides to a stop. Used for every move, no exceptions.
In
900 ms. Fade up 14 px from below with a light blur that clears.
Out
600 ms. Same curve, reversed: fade down and blur out.
Stagger
150 ms between pieces: card, then kicker, then headline.
Hold
At least 2 s fully still before it leaves.
Never
Bounces, spins, zoom punches, or two things moving in different ways.
