## 2. Crypto Group

### Who they are
People willing to take a small risk to invest early into a new financial structure, open to learn how to be sovereign in their financing, and take control of their financial lives to set them up for true wealth in the future. These might be opportunity seekers, or business entrepreneurs.

### The problem I solve
Teaching someone the basics of investing, how to move and use crypto, how to use a DEX, and wth a DEX even is, and more information around financial sovereignty, cold storage, web 3 wallets, and more. But not specifically one on one trading.

### Offer
- Free tier
- Level 1: **$44.44/mo**

### Where they are
Right now, only people I know personally. Zero signups from the landing page so far. Likely spots: Discord, Telegram, and the audience of daily crypto update channels like Crypto Banter.

### Pain points
- "wth a DEX even is."
- Don't know how to move crypto off an exchange, or which wallet to use.
- Overwhelmed by seed phrases, networks, and cold storage.

### Fears
- Losing funds by sending to the wrong network. Sources call it one of the most painful crypto mistakes, because transactions are irreversible.
- Losing or leaking the seed phrase. Every wallet has a 12 or 24 word seed phrase that controls it.
- Getting scammed. No legitimate support team will ask for your seed phrase.
- Being overwhelmed. One seed-storage writer: **"if you're afraid of everything, you're overwhelmed."**

### Dreams / goals
- Get in early on a new financial structure.
- Hold their own keys. Financial sovereignty.
- True wealth in the future, not living paycheck to paycheck in the 9-5.

### Who they are
Business owners who want better online presence, social media posts, engagement, visuals, editing, or live video production, and who are currently failing at it. Mostly greater Orange County; editing and remote services are open to the whole USA. Restaurants are a core niche.

### The problem I solve

Most business owners are so busy trying to run their business that they forgot all about marketing and growth, or even have a clue that their business presence sucks. So Marketing strategy, consultations, and implementation solves this.

### Offer
- **$1,000/mo minimum** engagement
- Bespoke videos and campaigns: a few thousand dollars, depending on scope

### Where they are
LinkedIn, Facebook, and local OC.

### Pain points
- Too busy running the business to market it.
